# Yeshua Roots — Community Participation and Safety Charter

**Status:** Stage 3 preparation. Community accounts, posts, prayer requests, and moderation tools are **not yet live**. This charter is a proposed operational policy, not a founder-approved Statement of Faith.

## Purpose

Our future community will help Jewish and Gentile followers and seekers learn about Yeshua, the living Jewish Messiah of Israel and Savior of the world, through Scripture, prayer, and respectful fellowship. The Jewish context of Scripture is honored without replacing Israel or requiring uniformity on every interpretive question.

## Participation standards

1. **Respect people.** No antisemitism, racism, dehumanization, threats, harassment, or attacks on Jewish or Gentile identity. Do not mock a person's religious background.
2. **Discuss Scripture responsibly.** Cite passages and distinguish the biblical text, historical context, and your interpretation. Disagreement is welcome when expressed without personal attacks.
3. **No coercion.** Do not pressure members to disclose religious identity, private history, contact details, or prayer needs. No unwanted proselytizing by direct message.
4. **Protect privacy.** Do not publish someone else's personal information, photos, correspondence, location, or prayer request without clear consent. Prayer requests should default to private until the author explicitly chooses an audience.
5. **No exploitation.** No fundraising solicitations, financial scams, spam, impersonation, or claims that donating purchases divine favor.
6. **Handle urgent situations safely.** Community moderators are not emergency responders or licensed clinicians. Threats of immediate harm should be directed to local emergency services; no promise of continuous monitoring.
7. **Keep minors safe.** No adult-to-minor private messaging or collection of children's sensitive data without an appropriate verified safeguarding program. Do not launch such features before safety review.

## Moderation principles

- Provide visible report and block controls before enabling user-generated content.
- Apply a graduated response when safe: reminder, content removal, temporary restriction, or suspension. Serious threats, doxxing, or exploitation can justify immediate restriction.
- Keep moderator access limited, record decisions, and provide a route to appeal decisions without exposing reporter identities.
- Clearly label community posts as user contributions, not official Yeshua Roots teaching. Founder doctrinal approval applies only to exact reviewed ministry teaching.
- Require explicit consent for public congregation directory listings; provide correction and removal requests.
- Do not launch prayer posting or messaging without privacy controls, abuse handling, retention/deletion rules, and a designated moderation owner.

## Hebrew / English parity

Provide community rules, reporting guidance, and moderation notices in both English and Hebrew. Support right-to-left text and accessible controls. Enforce the same standards regardless of language.

## Launch gates

- [ ] Founder reviews and approves the public community policy.
- [ ] Authentication, account deletion, and privacy notices are implemented and tested.
- [ ] Reporting, blocking, rate limits, and moderator tooling work end-to-end.
- [ ] Prayer requests have consent-based visibility and deletion controls.
- [ ] Moderation coverage and escalation ownership are assigned.
- [ ] Abuse and accessibility tests pass in English and Hebrew.

Until these gates are met, community functionality remains labeled **in development**.
