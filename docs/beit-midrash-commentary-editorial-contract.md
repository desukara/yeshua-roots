# Beit Midrash commentary editorial contract

Status: **editorial foundation only**. This file does not authorize publication of any new commentary.

## Study entry requirements

Each study must contain:

- **Reference:** canonical book, chapter and verse range; identify the Scripture translation displayed.
- **Jewish commentary:** a traceable primary source (author, work, location, edition or stable URL, original-language quotation when permitted), a faithful summary explicitly identified as a summary, and a reviewer verification record. Never fabricate or paraphrase as a direct quotation.
- **Messianic reflection:** separately labeled original editorial interpretation connecting the passage to Yeshua, with explicit cross-references. Do not attribute this reflection to historical Jewish commentators.
- **Publication status:** draft → source-verified → doctrinally-reviewed → founder-approved → published. No automatic publication from drafts.
- **Languages:** independently reviewed English and modern Israeli Hebrew copy, with RTL checks and appropriate language/direction attributes.
- **Fallback:** if an approved study is unavailable, show Scripture with a truthful notice; do not imply that commentary exists below.

## Yeshua Roots identity: kosher and Messiah-affirming

Yeshua Roots is a **kosher, Torah-honoring, Yeshua-centered** ministry. Its editorial voice affirms Yeshua as the living Jewish Messiah of Israel. No published teaching may deny His Messiahship, belittle keeping kosher, present kosher observance as spiritually inferior, or imply that following Yeshua requires abandoning kosher practice. Explain the Torah's food distinctions in their scriptural context. When citing Acts 10, include Peter's explanation about people in Acts 10:28 rather than using the vision as an unsupported blanket dismissal of kashrut.

Historical Jewish interpretations must remain accurately attributed without pretending their authors confessed Yeshua. Messianic conclusions must be labeled as Yeshua Roots' own reading. These are separate obligations: source fidelity and confessional clarity.

## Founder-directed rabbinic and Messianic-era review standard (2026-10-26)

- **Retain Jewish rabbinic scholarship** as historical, linguistic, legal, and cultural evidence. Do not delete or reject an entire source tradition simply because its author was not Messianic.
- **Strict Messiah-affirming publication screening:** do not publish a rabbinic excerpt, summary, featured quotation, or featured outbound passage that explicitly denies, disparages, attacks, **or reasonably implies a denial of** Yeshua's Messiahship, including indirect claims when read in their original context. Screen both the excerpt and surrounding argument in each display language. If the meaning is ambiguous or cannot be verified, **withhold it from publication** pending a documented decision. Mere silence about Yeshua is not by itself denial; evaluate what the passage actually communicates.
- **Source fidelity is mandatory:** never silently change, censor inside a quotation, or falsely attribute a Messianic confession to a rabbinic author. When the historical source anticipates a Messiah yet to come, describe that author's actual expectation accurately and keep it separate from the ministry's interpretation.
- **Yeshua Roots' separate, labeled Messianic reflection:** affirm Yeshua's first coming as Israel's promised Messiah, His resurrection and salvation for believers; interpret future Messianic coming in light of His return and gathering of believers to Himself; explain His Kingdom as already present through the Holy Spirit and His future millennial reign from Jerusalem. Use specific Scripture citations for each interpretive claim and distinguish theological inference from the source's own words.
- **No uncontrolled public API:** Scripture endpoints may serve biblical text without third-party commentary. Any future rabbinic API must be an explicit allowlist of individually source-verified, licensed, bilingual-reviewed, founder-approved excerpts. Reject unknown references and deny unreviewed content; do not proxy arbitrary external texts or return unfiltered upstream commentary.
- **No implicit approval:** this standard does not approve existing draft excerpts. Source and translation rights, explicit-and-implicit Messiahship screening, and founder approval are required per excerpt before enabling public delivery. Do not 'fix' an incompatible excerpt by adding a Messianic footnote: exclude the excerpt instead. A separate Messianic reflection is appropriate only alongside an otherwise acceptable, faithfully represented historical passage.

## Release gate

Do not ship substantive commentary until citations, translation permissions, accuracy, bilingual review and founder approval are documented. This is separate from deploying the Phase 3 reader foundation.
