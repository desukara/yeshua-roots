## Genesis 1:1

- **Jewish source:** Rashi on Genesis 1:1, opening comment, beginning “בראשית. אמר רבי יצחק”.
- **Primary text:** https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Genesis.1.1.1
- **Verification:** Rashi reports Rabbi Isaac's question about beginning with Exodus 12:2; cites Psalm 111:6; describes the Creator's ownership of the earth and the land's allotment. The draft is an original summary, not a quotation.
- **Messianic reading:** editorial connection to John 1:1–5 and Colossians 1:15–17. Not attributed to Rashi.

## Leviticus 11:10

- **Jewish source:** Mishnah Chullin 3:7, section beginning “וּבַדָּגִים”.
- **Primary text:** https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Chullin.3.7
- **Verification:** Mishnah describes permitted fish by fins and scales and records Rabbi Judah's two-scales/one-fin criterion. The draft is an original summary, not a quotation.
- **Messianic reading:** editorial reading of Acts 10:28, honoring kosher observance and not conflating Peter's explanation about people with the Torah's dietary distinctions.
