# Tokyo Outreach — Partner Contact Brief

**Internal planning only.** No organization listed here has agreed to partner with Yeshua Roots. No outreach service, public assistance intake, fundraising, or volunteer deployment is active.

## Priority: Sanyūkai (山友会), Sanya / Taitō

Official page: https://www.sanyukai.or.jp/volunteer-takidasi-outreach

- Contact: info@sanyukai.or.jp; 03-3874-1269.
- Location: Kiyokawa 2-32-8, Taitō-ku, Tokyo.
- Official page describes Thursday meal preparation, food distribution by the Sumida River, and visits to people living in tents.
- They seek people who can participate regularly (weekly or every other week); applicants should contact the organization and arrange an introductory meeting.
- Ask whether an individual Yeshua Roots representative can first attend an orientation, what the volunteer prerequisites are, and whether any future collaboration is appropriate.
- Do **not** assume a group can show up without registration or bring independently prepared food.

### TENOHASI (てのはし), Ikebukuro

Official site: https://www.tenohasi.or.jp/

- Existing meal distribution, outreach, and referral support.
- Confirm current volunteer orientation, capacity, and requirements through official channels before any visit.

## Initial contact questions

1. Are new individual volunteers or small groups currently accepted?
2. Is Japanese proficiency necessary for the activity?
3. Is there an orientation, screening, insurance, or minimum ongoing commitment?
4. What items or labor are most needed, and what should volunteers *not* bring?
5. Are there guidelines on photography, beneficiary privacy, religious activity, and child safeguarding?
6. Would you be open to a later conversation about a no-fee, no-pressure community service partnership?

## Safe pilot criteria

- Written permission or registration with host organization before attending.
- At least one trained point of contact and an emergency procedure.
- No collection of beneficiary names, photos, immigration details, or medical histories by Yeshua Roots.
- No religious condition on receiving food or assistance.
- No claims of partnership, impact numbers, or donation use until verified.
- No donation intake until financial and legal safeguards are ready.
